2.Revenue and age correlation
	1. revenue sweet spot:Both "sum of monthly charges by age" and "count of churn_new by age" shows a bell shaped curve which means normal distribution peaks
	between the ages of 40 and 60. This tells us that middle aged customers are highly specific source of revenue and this age group most likely to churn so 
	company should take retention efforts particularly for this segement.
	2.Lifetime value: There is a stark difference in "max of total charges by churn" chart.Customers who do not churn have higher maximum total charges compared 
	to those who do. This is because the longer a customer stays(their tenure),the more monthly charges they accumulate,thereby increasing their overall lifetime
	value to the company.
3.Contract and service trends
	1. contract composition:The pie chart for "sum of Total charges by contract type" shows a mix of Month-to-Month,one-year,and two year contracts.
	while month-to-month is a large segment.
	2.service impact:"The count of internet service by churn" represents that customer base is split between Fibre optic,DSL,and those with no internet service.

SUMMARY:Retention strategies should likely focus on the 40-60 age demographic as they are highest contributors to monthly revenue but also show high churn counts.
	Additionally,shifting customers from month-to-month to longer-term contracts can be explored to mirror the higher total charges seen in non-churning customers.
